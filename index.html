Our Story

A decade of moments that matter

Founded in 2015, Purple Ribbons by Amy began with a simple belief: every celebration deserves to feel intentional, personal, and effortlessly beautiful. What started as a passion for transforming spaces has grown into Lagos' trusted name in event planning and decoration.

Over ten years, we've had the privilege of designing atmospheres for intimate gatherings and grand celebrations alike—from the quiet elegance of a bridal shower to the vibrant energy of a corporate gala. Each event carries its own story, and our work is to give that story a setting worthy of it.
